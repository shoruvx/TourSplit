# TourSplit Changelog

All notable changes to TourSplit are documented in this file. Releases follow a detailed yet minimalistic format highlighting core capabilities, bug fixes, and user experience enhancements.

---

## [v1.4.1] - 2026-09-14

- 👤 **Member Profile Hub**: Tap any member avatar or name anywhere in the app to view their profile, role, contact info, and tour activity.
- ⚡ **Offline Auto-Settlement**: Settlements with offline companions now resolve automatically without requiring approval from the offline friend.
- 🎨 **3D Teal UI & System Theme**: Smooth 3D elevation cards with sleek teal borders, white-bordered buttons, and adaptive system default theme.
- 📊 **Your Spending Breakdown**: Tours and summary cards now display your exact personal spending share rather than a generic per-person average.
- 📬 **Contact Us & Suggestion Portal**: Send feature ideas, feedback, and bug reports directly to Constant Time Labs from the Profile screen.
- 🚀 **Over-The-Air Push Updates**: Stay up-to-date with background push alerts via Firebase Cloud Messaging and 1-tap seamless in-app APK installation.
- 🔄 **Dynamic What's New Dialog**: Automatically presents update highlights on first launch after an update with refined scrolling and layouts.

---

## [v1.4.0] - 2026-09-14

- 👤 **Member Profile Hub**: Dedicated member profile screen showing individual spending, total contributions, settlement status, and contact actions.
- ⚡ **Offline Auto-Settlement**: Automated balance settlement resolution for offline travel companions without blocking pending approvals.
- 🎨 **3D Teal Design System**: Elevated card aesthetics with teal border accents, contrasting light/dark styles, and system theme auto-detection.
- 📊 **Personal Spending Share**: Instant breakdown of personal expense share across tours and dashboard summary widgets.
- 📬 **Constant Time Labs Feedback**: Integrated Contact Us portal to submit bug reports, suggestions, and feature requests.
- 🚀 **Firebase Cloud Messaging OTA**: Seamless over-the-air update notifications with direct background APK download and prompt.

---

## [v1.3.0] - 2026-09-11

- 📊 **Trip Summaries at a Glance**: See total spending, cost per person, member count, and expense count right on every tour card without opening it.
- 💳 **1-Tap Payout Accounts**: Save your receiving accounts (bKash, Nagad, Rocket, Bank) in your profile. During settlements, friends can tap once to copy your number with instant tactile feedback.
- 🎨 **Fresh App Icon & Splash**: Redesigned modern TourSplit icon and a clean, seamless launch screen when opening the app.
- 👋 **Rotating Welcome Greetings**: A fresh, friendly greeting greets you each time you open the app.
- ⚡ **Smoother & Faster**: Polished layouts and performance improvements for effortless group expense tracking.

---

## [v1.2.0] - 2026-09-08

- 🧮 **In-Line Math Calculator**: Type arithmetic expressions directly into the expense amount box (e.g., `500 * 10`, `(50 * 5) + 34`). It automatically converts to the final total on blur, complete with a live preview badge and quick operator keypad.
- 🔗 **Link Offline Friends to Online Accounts**: Seamlessly replace offline friend placeholders with their real TourSplit accounts once they sign up. All historical expenses, shares, and settlements transfer automatically.
- 🖼️ **Real-Time Profile Picture Customization**: Change profile photos on the fly via Camera, Photo Gallery, or preset travel avatars with instant real-time sync across tour members.
- ⚖️ **Settlement Alignment & Dialog Polish**: Fixed the vertical letter-wrapping bug on settlement cards and restructured the "Pay Full Due" dialog to prevent layout collapse.
- 🧭 **Intuitive Tour Navigation**: Tapping the tour title instantly opens All Tours, while pressing back returns straight to the 3-option home screen (Create, Join, Browse).

---

## [v1.1.0] - 2026-09-06

- 📊 **Custom Split Spending Tracking**: Accurate personal spending calculations and balance breakdowns when members pay unequal or custom amounts.
- 👥 **Multi-Payer Expense Logging**: Support for multiple contributors splitting single bills with exact contribution logging.
- 🔘 **Enlarged Action Toggles**: Larger, touch-friendly split toggles and streamlined category selectors for faster expense logging on the move.
- 📜 **Tour Member Retention & History**: Safely retain past members' spending records in the tour ledger even if they leave the tour.
- 💵 **Direct Manual Settlement**: Streamlined manual settlement entry with quick prefilled due balances.

---

## [v1.0.8] - 2026-09-04

- 🧹 **Clear Balances & Manual Settlement**: Direct balance clearing and settlement workflow improvements.
- 🔔 **Automatic Update System**: Integrated GitHub release checker for instant update prompts.
