🗺️ TourSplit — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

1. Introduction

TourSplit ("we", "our", or "the app") is a tour expense splitting application developed by Shoruv. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use TourSplit.

2. Information We Collect

  • Google Account Information: Your name, email address, and profile photo when you sign in with Google.
  • Tour & Expense Data: Tour names, expense records, member lists, categories, and settlement information you create within the app.
  • Device Information: Basic device information for app functionality and crash reporting.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and operate the TourSplit service
  • Sync your tour and expense data in real-time across all members' devices
  • Identify you to other tour members you invite
  • Improve app performance and fix issues

4. Data Storage & Security

All data is stored securely using Google Firebase Firestore. Your data is protected by Google's enterprise-grade security infrastructure and Firestore security rules that restrict access to authorized tour members only. We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with any third parties.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase: Authentication and cloud database — Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google Sign-In: User authentication — Google Privacy Policy

6. Data Sharing

Your expense and tour data is shared only with the members you explicitly invite to your tour using the invite code or QR code. We do not share your data with any other third parties.

7. Data Deletion

You can delete your tour data at any time from within the app (Admin → Tour Settings → Delete Tour). To request complete account deletion or data removal, contact us at the email below and we will process your request within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

TourSplit is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any significant changes through an in-app update notice. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
