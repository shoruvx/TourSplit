TourSplit is a smart, real-time travel expense tracker, debt settlement, and hybrid collaboration application built for real trips with real people.

Whether you are traveling with companions, sharing an apartment with roommates, or organizing group activities, TourSplit makes tracking shared spending effortless, transparent, and completely confusion-free.

Key Features:

• Real-Time & Offline Expense Tracking
Record shared expenses as they happen. Offline storage powered by Hive ensures complete usability even without an active cellular or internet connection.

• Greedy Debt Simplification
Our two-pointer greedy debt reduction algorithm collapses multi-party liabilities into the absolute minimum number of settlement transfers.

• Peer-to-Peer Offline Sync
Collaborate off-grid using nearby Bluetooth mesh networking when traveling through remote areas with zero cellular reception.

• In-Line Math Calculator
Write math expressions directly into the expense amount field (e.g. 500 * 2 + 350) with instant glowing preview total calculation.

• Offline Companions & Account Migration
Add friends with just a name in seconds even if they haven't installed the app, and seamlessly link their profiles once they join.

• Itemized PDF Reports & Export
Generate clean, professional accounting summaries and debt settlement breakdowns ready to print or share with one tap.

• Multi-Currency & Settlement Channels
Track transactions across multiple currencies and record settlements through cash, bank transfer, and mobile payment channels.
